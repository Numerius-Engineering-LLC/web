    <h2>Project focus</h2>
    <p>This was my first mechanical engineering project in college. As team leader, I guided a group of students through designing and building a complete vehicle for the SAE-sponsored Mini Baja event, in which student teams build small off-road cars around a lawn mower engine.</p>
    <p>The finished car has a tubular roll cage, aluminum body panels, a seat with a racing harness, a fire extinguisher mounted for safety, and independent suspension with coil-over shocks at each wheel.</p>
    <h2>What it demonstrates</h2>
    <p>Beyond the mechanical design and fabrication, this project taught me a great deal about project management for a medium-sized team. Coordinating many people and parts toward one working vehicle was an early and lasting lesson in planning and leading engineering work.</p>
