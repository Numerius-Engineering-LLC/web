    <h2>Project focus</h2>
    <p>The goal was a product that students could make cheaply and that people would want to buy. The design is built from flat plywood parts with notched joints and decorative curved cut-outs, so it uses simple materials and straightforward cuts while still looking finished. The result is a table with two benches, and it was sold in a fundraiser for the school's homecoming.</p>
    <h2>What it demonstrates</h2>
    <p>This was where my engineering career began. It was my first experience designing a real product around real constraints: cost, ease of fabrication, a buyer, and a purpose beyond the design itself. Thinking about how a product will be made while designing it is something I still do in all my work.</p>
