  <article class="content-block">
    <h2>Project focus</h2>
    <p>The toy is a tower of ramps and tracks for marbles, built from 10 interlocking pieces. Designing it meant making every piece fit its neighbors cleanly as part of one assembly.</p>
    <p>To make the design suitable for mass production, I adapted each piece for injection molding and handled the tolerancing, so that parts made in a mold would assemble reliably.</p>
    <h2>What it demonstrates</h2>
    <p>This project was my first serious step into CAD. It covers designing an assembly of interlocking parts, designing for manufacturing, and specifying tolerances so that fit, function, and moldability all work together in a product made for children.</p>
