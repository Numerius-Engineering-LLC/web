  <h1>Catamaran Pencil Holder</h1>
  <p class="lead">A sailboat-themed desk organizer designed in CAD and 3D printed as a gift for my dad, and the first product I designed and printed entirely myself.</p>
</section>

  <article class="content-block">
    <h2>Project focus</h2>
    <p>The design takes the form of a catamaran: two yellow hulls carrying a navy deck and a white sail. The deck is shaped around its contents, with a cradle for a roll of tape, a place for pens and pencils, and room for small supplies, so the object works as a desk organizer as well as a sailboat.</p>
    <p>It was a Father's Day present, and the first time I took an idea from a CAD model to a finished print of my own design.</p>
    <h2>What it demonstrates</h2>
    <p>This project shows the product design process from the user's side: choosing a form that people enjoy, fitting real objects such as tape, pens, and glue into it, and building an assembly of several printed parts, in multiple colors, that looks like the boat it imitates.</p>
