    <h2>Project focus</h2>
    <p>The playhouse is a raised, roofed platform with slatted guard walls. A ladder leads up on one side and a slide leaves from the other, and the open space beneath the deck adds room to play. Every board, post, and rung was modeled in CAD before any lumber was cut, so the structure could be checked and the parts planned in advance.</p>
    <p>The finished playhouse was then built in the backyard from lumber, following the model.</p>
    <h2>What it demonstrates</h2>
    <p>This project carries a design all the way from a computer model to a full-size structure that children use. It combines CAD design with practical construction and carpentry skills: planning lumber and framing, building a raised platform with a roof, and designing for the safety and enjoyment of its users.</p>
