    <h2>Project focus</h2>
    <p>This project was mostly for fun. The goal was a printed mount that holds a laser module securely and in line with the barrel of a toy blaster, so that the beam shows where the toy is aimed. It is a small exercise in fitting a printed part around an existing object and a purchased component.</p>
    <h2>What it demonstrates</h2>
    <p>It shows how quickly a simple idea can become a working fixture with 3D printing. Lasers are also a part of my professional work, where I have experience with high-power laser systems, so this project connects a personal hobby to the kind of optical work I do professionally.</p>
